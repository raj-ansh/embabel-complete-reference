# NorthWind Returns Policy

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Gold members have 60 days.
Items must be unused, with original tags and packaging.

## Damaged or faulty items

If an item arrives damaged or develops a fault, you can return it within 90 days of delivery for a full
refund or a replacement, whichever you prefer. Please include a photo of the damage when you contact us.

## Items that cannot be returned

Final-sale (clearance) items, gift cards and personalised products cannot be returned unless faulty.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 working days of the return being received.
Return shipping is free for damaged or faulty items; otherwise a 6.50 return label is deducted.
