# NorthWind Gear Warranty

## What the warranty covers

All NorthWind-branded gear has a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.
Waterproof boots have a 1-year waterproofing guarantee: if they leak in normal use, we repair or replace them.

## What the warranty does not cover

Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, accidents, and alterations are not covered. Zips and buckles
damaged by force are not covered, but we offer low-cost repairs.

## Making a warranty claim

Contact customer care with your order number and photos. If the claim is accepted we send a prepaid
label; repairs take about 10 working days, replacements ship within 2 working days.
