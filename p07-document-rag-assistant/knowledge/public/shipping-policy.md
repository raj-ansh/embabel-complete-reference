# NorthWind Shipping Policy

## Delivery zones and times

Zone 1 (mainland) orders arrive in 2 working days. Zone 2 (islands and remote areas) orders arrive in 4
working days. Orders placed before 2pm ship the same day.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping costs 6.50. Orders over 75 ship free. Gold members always get free shipping.

## Tracking your parcel

Every order gets a tracking number starting with PF- by e-mail when it ships. Parcels can be tracked on
the carrier's website or by asking our assistant.

## Oversized items

Tents over 10 kg and kayaks ship by freight, which takes 5 to 7 working days and requires a signature.
