# Staff Guide: Refund Authority

## Who can approve refunds

Customer care agents may issue refunds up to 100 without approval. Refunds from 100 to 1,000 need a
support lead. Refunds above 1,000 need the customer care manager.

## Goodwill gestures

Agents may offer a goodwill voucher of up to 15 for delays caused by NorthWind. Vouchers are never offered
for carrier delays outside our control.

## Fraud warning signs

Escalate to the fraud team if a customer requests more than three refunds in 30 days, asks for refunds to a
different card, or reports non-delivery for a parcel the carrier shows as signed for.
