# Lumen 400 Headlamp Manual

## Modes

Press once for low (40 lumens, 60 hours), twice for medium (150 lumens, 12 hours), three times for high
(400 lumens, 3 hours). Hold for 2 seconds to switch to the red night-vision light.

## Charging

Charge through the USB-C port. A full charge takes 3 hours; the indicator turns green when complete.
Charge only between 0 °C and 40 °C.

## Lock mode

To stop the lamp switching on in your pack, hold the button for 5 seconds until it flashes twice. Repeat to
unlock.

## Error: lamp flashes red three times

Three red flashes mean the battery is too cold or too low to run on high. Warm the lamp, charge it, or use
low or medium mode.
