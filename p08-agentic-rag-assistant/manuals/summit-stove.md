# Summit Canister Stove Manual

## Safety

Never use the stove inside a tent, porch or other enclosed space: it produces carbon monoxide.
Keep it at least 1 metre from the tent and from anything flammable. Let it cool before packing.

## Lighting the stove

Screw the stove onto a compatible EN417 gas canister hand-tight. Open the valve a quarter turn and press the
piezo igniter. If the igniter fails, close the valve, wait 30 seconds, and light with a match held to the side
of the burner.

## Low flame in cold weather

Gas pressure drops below 0 °C. Keep the canister warm in your sleeping bag overnight, use a winter-blend
canister, and shield the stove from wind with the folding windscreen.

## Error: yellow, sooty flame

A yellow flame means the jet is partially blocked or the canister is nearly empty. Let the stove cool,
clean the jet with the pricker tool, and try a new canister.
