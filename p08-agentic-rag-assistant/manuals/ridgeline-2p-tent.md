# Ridgeline 2-Person Tent Manual

## Pitching the tent

Lay the inner tent flat with the door facing away from the wind. Insert the two colour-coded poles into the
matching sleeves, then clip the inner to the poles. Peg the four corners at 45 degrees, pulling the floor taut.

## Fitting the flysheet

Throw the flysheet over the frame with the door over the inner door. Fasten the buckles at each corner and
tension the guy lines. A gap of 5 cm between flysheet and inner prevents condensation from soaking through.

## Condensation

Condensation forms on the inside of the flysheet when warm, moist air meets the cold fabric. It is not a leak.
Open both vents, avoid cooking inside, and pitch away from rivers and dips where cold air collects.

## Repairing a torn pole sleeve or a split pole

Use the aluminium repair sleeve in the stuff sack: slide it over the split section and tape both ends.
Contact NorthWind repairs for a replacement pole section; poles are covered by the 2-year warranty.
