# Writing a returns refusal letter

1. Thank the customer by name and restate what they asked for in one sentence.
2. Give the reason for the refusal, quoting the policy clause from the facts you were given. Never invent a clause.
3. Offer the next best option: repair, store credit, or the manufacturer's warranty.
4. Close with the support team's name, never an individual's.

Follow the tone guide in references/tone.md.
